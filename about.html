CMI 水灯节庆典 × 妙游记：照见不同的人生

亲手做一盏水灯，和妙游记的伙伴交换故事，再一起做饭、点灯，把一个下午和夜晚留给彼此。这次有两种体验方式：庆典体验 + 共创者，完整参加人生交换；水灯体验 + 共创者，人生交换可选。你可以按自己的意愿选择。

01 今年，为什么在这里相聚

从一起出去过节，到一起创造庆典

水灯节是泰国最重要的节日之一，传统上在泰历十二月月圆之夜举行。人们把水灯放到水面上，感恩水的恩赐，也放下怨气和不顺。这往往也是清迈最热闹的季节正式到来的时候。一年一次的水灯节，也串起了 CMI 社区一年又一年不同的画面。

往年，CMI 社区的 Base 在清迈客栈。每一年，我们都和从全国各地、全球各地来到清迈的华人数字游民小伙伴们一起，从清迈客栈出发，参与到泰国的庆祝活动之中，一起融入全城的盛景，也越来越融入当地：

  • 2024 年，我们在清迈古城、湄平河边参加放水灯。

  • 2025 年，我们去了以放天灯闻名的素可泰古城。

这几年里，CMI 社区也在慢慢成长，遇见了越来越多的人，也经历了越来越多的事。

今年，CMI 社区从古城搬到了清迈沙拉坯县。这里有：

  • 宽广的草地；

  • 自己的水塘；

  • 独立的社区活动空间；

  • 自然风景环绕的环境。

我们一抬头就能看到蓝天和星星，我们可以发出最大的声音，也不会打扰到他人。

从 2023 年到现在，这是 CMI 社区过的第四个水灯节。所以今年，我们希望创造一次不一样的社区水灯节体验。我们希望这是一次由大家共同创造、共同建设出来的，独属于这个社区、独属于我们之间的一次水灯节和天灯节体验。

这次，与妙游记相遇

今年的水灯节非常有幸，我们不只有自己，还有来自「妙游记」综艺旅行活动的十多位参与者，在 CMI Studio 与我们共同度过。

他们是第一次来到泰国的旅客，也是一群有着独特人生经历的人：

  • 来自各行各业；

  • 对世界充满好奇；

  • 有丰富的人生经历；

  • 想看看不一样的世界，和更多的可能性建立连接。

他们的共同之处在于：每个人都有自己独特的人生故事。

这也正是过去两年、每年一次的合作中，我们欣赏妙游记的一点：他们选择的参与者，都有着：

  • 对世界的好奇；

  • 探索生活的热情；

  • 各自丰富的人生经历。

今年，已经是妙游记第三年带队来到 CMI 社区。但并不是每一次都过水灯节或天灯节：每一次合作的主题都不一样，而今年，是第一次和我们一起过水灯节。我们在以社区之间共同创作、共同发起的形式，打造不一样的社区体验。

说来也巧，CMI 社区 2022 年在清迈创立，已经持续存在了四年，有非常多很有意思的伙伴。所以今年我们一拍即合，共同策划了 11 月 22 日水灯节庆典的核心主题：照见不同的人生故事。

今年，CMI 社区为水灯节庆典赋予的主题是：在 CMI 点亮自制水灯，照见不同人生，共创节日庆典。

为什么想在此刻了解彼此

我们在祖国之外，深刻地感受着时代的变化落在每个人身上的影响。

特别是当下这个时候，我们因为相隔国界，生活产生了太多的不同，同一个世界被过出了不同的体验。我们看似处处不同，却生活在共同的社会背景之下。

大时代下的每个人：

  • 如何生活？

  • 如何生存？

  • 如何看待这个时代？

想要搞清楚这些问题，就需要无数次的线下编织，让人与人、心与心走到一起，看到我们每个人之间到底相距多远。

自从来到海外，我们一直在过一种拼拼凑凑的生活：

  • 拼凑我们对世界的认识；

  • 拼凑我们共同的生活；

  • 一点点拼凑出我们自己的样子。

有时候，看见彼此只需要你开始交谈。我们的人生在这个世界上本来可能并不相干，但在这个一切都被加速、虚拟世界占据了那么多时光的日子里，我们还愿意在线下和真实的人面对面，这何尝不是一件美妙的事呢？

在这个可以自由游牧的时代，我们可以做到这件事。不是通过社交媒体，不是隔着屏幕，而是有机会在线下，在 CMI 社区的场域里，把我们的人生深度地传递给彼此。

所以这次与妙游记共创的现场，我们想让大家感受到的体验关键词是：剪贴、交织、共建。这三个词，落在这一天里实实在在要做的事情上：

  • 每个人的人生故事在现场交换，从一个人的心走到另一个人的心里；

  • 水灯的材料在现场折叠、拼接，从一堆材料变成我们一起拼出来的样子；

  • 食材从各个地方来到现场，由来自世界各地的手一起做成空想食堂的样子；

  • 最后，带着接收到的故事、传递出去的祝福和饱饱的幸福感，一起点亮水灯，让它飘在水面上。

在这个过程中，没有人是别人的消费品。我们每个人都是这个过程和结果的一部分，也是在为自己深入连接这个世界，贡献一份力量。

02 全天流程体验

11 月 22 日，14:00 到 21:00，这一天从白天走进夜晚，一层一层叠起来。

  • 做一盏灯。

  • 换一段人生。

  • 同桌吃一顿饭。

  • 放走一份祝福。

入场 · 破冰

暖洋洋的午后走进 CMI，彩旗、竹桌和笑脸在草坪上等你。我们在门口迎接每一位朋友：

  • 喝口水；

  • 认识一下身边的人；

  • 看看今天的场地。

开场里，一人一句「我是谁」，往年水灯节和天灯的天空，一张张翻过。

亲手做水灯

坐在草坪的垫子上，和刚到的新朋友一起折蕉叶、插鲜花，用最解压的方式感受动手的快乐。

现场会有兰纳文化深度研究者，用中文分享水灯背后的文化传承与历史故事，并带领大家发挥创意、动手制作。

创意材料都已备好，一堆材料在大家手里折叠、拼接，变成一盏属于这一天的水灯，动手间就认识了彼此。

要放进灯里的东西，不必提前决定。

妙游记人生交换

节奏慢下来，围坐成圈。和来自各行各业的妙游记伙伴面对面，与一位现在还是陌生人的朋友，建立一段有趣的连接。不隔着屏幕，听见另一种活法。

在人生交换里，我们可以：

  • 互换一段人生经历，听见彼此的故事；

  • 交换一份独特的技能，分享各自的生活经验；

  • 了解另一种身份、另一种人生是怎样的体验；

  • 分享那些在清迈才理解的事情、才开始学会的道理和神奇的经历。

你带来的故事会走进另一个人的心里，你也会带走一个新的故事。让我们的故事、我们的看法，在 11 月 22 日的 CMI Studio 线下流动起来。

交换出去的，也许比你想的留得更久。

黄昏转场

天色变橘，灯串一盏盏亮起。草坪、鱼塘和远山迎来一天最美的光，拍下照片，看场地从白天走进夜晚。

空想食堂

天色暗下来以后，来自各地的食材，在来自世界各地的手里一起处理、一起下锅，变成同一张桌上的晚餐。

一起动手做饭、混坐开饭，一顿饭把大家的距离再拉近一点，不急着结束。

放水灯仪式

天黑了，烛光落进水面。带着听到的故事和想送出的祝福，点亮亲手做的那盏灯，从码头和塘边轻轻放走，看它慢慢飘远。

水灯承载的，是对未来的祝福。

篝火夜聊 · 收尾

篝火噼啪，灯串下围成一个个小圈子，放完灯，夜聊继续，不着急散。这一天，你不是看完一场活动，而是认识了一群人。

03 怎样参与体验

选择适合你的体验方式

这次有两种体验方式，区别在于人生交换的参与方式，其他体验相同。

庆典体验 + 共创者

  • 人生交换：完整参加故事与技能交换。

  • 体验重点：分享彼此的人生经历、技能，以及对世界的看法。

  • 名额安排：限量。

水灯体验 + 共创者

  • 人生交换：按自己的意愿选择是否参加。

  • 体验重点：以水灯手作为主，其他共同体验照常参加。

  • 名额安排：人数不限。

两种方式都包含的体验

  • 亲手做一盏自己的水灯；

  • 在空想食堂一起动手做饭、同桌吃饭；

  • 晚上一起点亮、放水灯；

  • 获得一件 CMI 社区 T 恤；

  • 获得两天后 CMI Studio 天灯节的入场。

费用：两种体验方式均为 200 铢/人（材料费 + 场地费）。

04 怎样参与共建

我们办这场活动，有两个目的：

  • 让远道而来的伙伴们，得到 CMI 社区热情的接待；

  • 让大家把水灯节庆典过成属于我们的共同体验，而不只是参与一场别人准备好的体验。

共创的过程，是把参加一场体验，变成一起创造一场体验的机会。一次次微小的彼此接住，都是我们共同的胜利，让 CMI 社区真正成为大家的社区。

活动前：前期共创筹划，自愿参与

无论选择哪种体验方式，都可以自愿参与前期筹备。你有想法，我们都特别支持。不必有整块的时间，每一点微小的帮助都欢迎。

为了让 CMI Studio 的现场氛围更好，这周的筹备可能需要大家一起搭把手。你可以按自己的意愿和时间选择：

  • 从家里带一两样你喜欢的小东西，用来装点现场或者自己的水灯；

  • 在活动群里看看这周有什么正好能搭把手的地方；

  • 帮忙制作、打印物料，采买当天要用的 DIY 材料，或者借出设备；

  • 活动当天提前来，帮忙布置现场。

所有准备的过程和讨论，我们都会在活动群里公开。如果正好有你能帮上忙的地方，CMI 社区非常愿意接受大家的帮助和共建。

活动当天：现场互助，力所能及

CMI 社区一直以来就是一个共建者社区，CMI Studio 的空间也一直开放给大家在现场自助使用。愿意帮忙的时候，可以从身边的小事做起：

  • 分享自己对现场空间的了解，帮有需要的人指路；

  • 告诉刚来的朋友哪里有水、哪里洗手、厕所在哪；

  • 提醒有需要的人怎么使用空间、遇到事情可以找谁；

  • 按自己的意愿，帮忙做一些现场协调。

现场有活、正好方便，就搭把手；空下来，就接着做你的水灯。

帮助出自本心

你来到现场，是和大家一起过节、相互认识。是否帮忙、怎样帮忙，都按自己的意愿和能力来。

我们不是在表演一种生活方式，而是用发自本心的善意对待彼此，敞开自己，去接纳和了解不同的人生样本。我相信，当你选择这样去做，你会有独特的收获。我们的每一份善意，也是在和所有人共同协作，让这场庆典真正成为我们共同的社区的庆典。

CMI 社区更倾向于用真心换真心的协作方式去建立信任，而不是博弈；不倾向于用经济、制度、条条框框为大家规定一条路径。

两天后，我们再一起放天灯

两种体验都包含这次天灯节入场。

水灯节两天后，我们在 CMI Studio 一起过天灯节。我们有一块属于自己的小天地，可以直接放天灯。不用挤人群，就是我们这群人，一起看着天灯慢慢飞上去。

05 写在最后

去年的水灯节，我们开着两辆皮卡，十几个人一起去了素可泰古城。素可泰最出名的，是放天灯。

那天晚上，成片的天灯从古老的遗迹之间升起来，铺满了整片夜空。我们站在下面，被那个画面震住了。双手合十许愿的那一刻，心里特别平静。

神奇的是，那年许下的愿望，很多真的都实现了！

今年，我们有了自己的草地和水塘，又有了一次难得的遇见。既然有这场庆典，那为什么不，我们一起试试呢？虽然准备时间已经很短了，但我相信，我们的相遇与协作，会让大家都有新的收获。相信我们每个人的善意，也相信我们彼此之间那份共同的信任。

想听听那些愿望背后的故事？那就来报名参加吧。

Local community × MeYouWorld, face to face | An immersive community experience for Loy Krathong

Once a year, a gathering to cherish. 🪷

Spend an unforgettable day at CMI Studio with the MeYouWorld guests who have just arrived in Chiang Mai. Make your own krathong, a floating lantern, exchange stories with friends from MeYouWorld, then cook and light your lanterns together. Set aside an afternoon and evening for one another.

Full Celebration + Co-creator — Full participation in Life Exchange.

Krathong Experience + Co-creator — Life Exchange is optional.

01 Why we are gathering here this year

From celebrating together elsewhere to creating a celebration in our own space

Loy Krathong is one of Thailand’s most important festivals, traditionally held on the full moon of the twelfth month of the Thai lunar calendar. People float krathongs on the water to give thanks for its gifts and let go of resentment and misfortune. It often marks the arrival of Chiang Mai’s liveliest season, too. For CMI Community, each year’s Loy Krathong has become another scene in our shared story.

In previous years, CMI Community was based at Chiang Mai Guesthouse. Every year, we would set off from the guesthouse with Chinese digital nomads who had come to Chiang Mai from across China and around the world. We joined Thailand’s celebrations, became part of the festivities across the city, and gradually grew closer to local life:

  • In 2024, we joined the krathong releases in Chiang Mai’s Old City and along the Ping River.

  • In 2025, we visited the ancient city of Sukhothai, known for its sky lantern releases.

Over these years, CMI Community has slowly grown, meeting more people and sharing more experiences along the way.

This year, CMI Community moved from the Old City to Saraphi District in Chiang Mai. Here, we have:

  • A wide, open lawn;

  • A pond of our own;

  • A dedicated space for community activities;

  • Natural scenery all around us.

We can look up and see blue skies and stars. We can be as loud as we like without disturbing anyone.

Since 2023, this will be CMI Community’s fourth Loy Krathong. This year, we hope to create a different kind of community celebration: a Loy Krathong and sky lantern experience that we create and build together, one that belongs to this community and to the connections between us.

Who’s joining us this year: friends from MeYouWorld

We are fortunate to have company for this year’s Loy Krathong: more than ten participants from MeYouWorld’s variety-show-style travel experience will join us at CMI Studio.

They are visiting Thailand for the first time, and each brings a distinctive life story:

  • They come from all walks of life;

  • They are curious about the world;

  • They have rich life experiences;

  • They want to see a different side of the world and connect with new possibilities.

What they share is this: every one of them has a life story of their own.

This is something we have come to appreciate about MeYouWorld through our annual collaborations over the past two years. The people they bring together all have:

  • Curiosity about the world;

  • Enthusiasm for exploring life;

  • A wealth of life experiences of their own.

This is the third year MeYouWorld has brought a group to CMI Community. These visits have not all been for Loy Krathong or the sky lantern festival: each collaboration has had a different theme. This will be their first Loy Krathong with us. By initiating and creating something together across our two communities, we are shaping a different kind of community experience.

As it happens, CMI Community was founded in Chiang Mai in 2022 and has now been around for four years, bringing together many fascinating people. So this year, we quickly found common ground and planned the central theme of our November 22 Loy Krathong celebration: illuminating different life stories.

This year, CMI Community’s theme for the celebration is: light your handmade krathong at CMI, discover different lives, and create a festival together.

Why we want to get to know one another now

Living outside our home country, we feel deeply how the changes of our time affect each person.

Especially now, living across national borders has made our lives so different. We inhabit the same world, yet experience it in different ways. For all our apparent differences, we still share a broader social context.

In times of great change, how does each of us:

  • Live our daily lives?

  • Make a living and get by?

  • Make sense of the times we live in?

To understand these questions, we need countless encounters in person, weaving connections between people and between hearts, and discovering how far apart we really are.

Since moving abroad, we have been piecing life together:

  • Piecing together our understanding of the world;

  • Piecing together a life we share;

  • Gradually piecing together who we are.

Sometimes, seeing one another begins with a conversation. Our lives might otherwise never have crossed. Yet in a time when everything moves faster and the virtual world takes up so much of our days, we are still willing to meet real people face to face. Isn’t that something wonderful?

In an age when we can roam freely, this is something we can do. Here at CMI Community, we have the chance to share our lives with one another in depth, in person, beyond social media and the screen.

For this gathering we are creating with MeYouWorld, the words we want people to experience are: collage, interweave, and build together. They take shape in the things we will actually do that day:

  • Life stories will be exchanged in person, finding their way from one heart to another;

  • Krathong materials will be folded and pieced together, becoming something we make as a group;

  • Ingredients will arrive from different places and be prepared by hands from around the world, bringing The Imaginary Kitchen to life;

  • Finally, carrying the stories we have received, the wishes we have shared, and the happiness of a full meal, we will light our krathongs together and set them afloat.

In this process, no one is someone else’s commodity. Each of us is part of both the making and what comes of it, contributing to our own deeper connection with the world.

A relaxing time to create

Sit on mats on the lawn, fold banana leaves, and arrange fresh flowers with your new friends. Enjoy the simple, soothing pleasure of making something with your hands.

Someone with an in-depth knowledge of Lanna culture will share the cultural traditions and historical stories behind krathongs in Chinese, then guide everyone as they get creative and make their own.

All the creative materials will be ready. As we fold and assemble them, a pile of materials will become a krathong that belongs to this day, and we will get to know one another as we work.

You do not have to decide in advance what you want to place inside your lantern.

MeYouWorld Life Exchange

Stories + skills

Slow down and sit in a circle. Meet friends from MeYouWorld from all walks of life, and make an interesting connection with someone who is still a stranger for now. Hear about another way of living, face to face, beyond the screen.

In Life Exchange, we can:

  • Exchange a chapter of our lives and hear one another’s stories;

  • Share a distinctive skill and what life has taught us;

  • Discover what it is like to live with a different identity or follow a different path;

  • Share the things we only came to understand in Chiang Mai, the lessons we began to learn here, and the extraordinary experiences we have had.

The story you bring will find a place in someone else’s heart, and you will leave with a new story, too. Let our stories and perspectives flow between us, in person, at CMI Studio on November 22.

What you share may stay with someone longer than you imagine.

Into dusk

The day’s most beautiful light

The sky turns orange, and strings of lights come on one by one. The lawn, fishpond, and distant hills catch the day’s most beautiful light. Take a few photos and watch the space move from day into night.

The Imaginary Kitchen

Cook together

As darkness falls, ingredients from different places will be prepared and cooked by hands from around the world, becoming dinner on one shared table.

Cook together, mix up the seating, and share a meal that brings us a little closer. There is no hurry to finish.

Krathong release

Send your wishes across the water

Night falls, and candlelight settles on the water. With the stories you have heard and the wishes you want to send, light the krathong you made, gently release it from the jetty or the pond’s edge, and watch it drift away.

Each krathong carries wishes for the future.

Fireside conversations · Closing

Leave knowing new people

The fire crackles, and small circles form under the string lights. After the lanterns have been released, the conversations continue, with no rush to leave. You will end the day having got to know a group of people, beyond simply attending an event.

03 Choose how to take part

Choose the experience that suits you

There are two ways to join. They differ in how you take part in Life Exchange; all other experiences are the same.

Full Celebration + Co-creator

  • Life Exchange: Take part fully in the exchange of stories and skills.

  • Focus: Share life experiences, skills, and perspectives on the world.

  • Places: Limited.

Krathong Experience + Co-creator

  • Life Exchange: Choose for yourself whether to take part.

  • Focus: Krathong making, with all the other shared experiences included as usual.

  • Places: No limit on participant numbers.

Both options include

  • Making your own krathong;

  • Cooking and sharing a meal at The Imaginary Kitchen;

  • Lighting and releasing krathongs together in the evening;

  • A CMI Community T-shirt;

  • Admission to the sky lantern festival at CMI Studio two days later.

Fee: Both options cost 200 THB per person (materials + venue).

04 Create the celebration together

We have two aims in bringing this event together:

  • To give our friends who have travelled from afar a warm CMI Community welcome;

  • To make Loy Krathong a shared experience that belongs to all of us, beyond taking part in something prepared by others.

Creating together turns attending an experience into a chance to help make it happen. Every small moment of showing up for one another is a shared success, helping CMI Community become a community that truly belongs to everyone.

Before the event: help plan and prepare, if you wish

Whichever experience you choose, you are welcome to volunteer in the preparations. We are happy to support your ideas. You do not need a large block of free time; even the smallest contribution is welcome.

We may need a few helping hands this week to make CMI Studio feel right for the day. Depending on your interests and availability, you could:

  • Bring one or two small things you like from home to decorate the space or your own krathong;

  • Check the event WeChat group to see whether there is anything you could help with this week;

  • Help make or print materials, buy DIY supplies for the day, or lend some equipment;

  • Come early on the day to help set up.

We will share the preparations and discussions openly in the event WeChat group. If there is something you can help with, CMI Community will gladly welcome your contribution.

On the day: help one another as you can

CMI Community has always been a community built by its members, and CMI Studio has always been open for everyone to use on a self-service basis. When you feel like helping, you can start with something small nearby:

  • Share what you know about the space and help someone find their way;

  • Show new arrivals where to get water, wash their hands, or find the toilets;

  • Explain how to use the space or who to ask when something comes up;

  • Help with coordination if you would like to.

If something needs doing and you have a moment, lend a hand. When you are free again, return to your krathong.

Help that comes from the heart

You are coming to celebrate together and get to know one another. Whether and how you help is entirely up to your wishes and abilities.

We want to treat one another with kindness that comes from the heart, rather than perform a lifestyle. We want to open ourselves to people whose lives have taken different paths. I believe that choosing to do this will bring you something uniquely your own. Every act of kindness is also a way of working together to make this celebration truly belong to our shared community.

At CMI Community, we prefer to build trust through sincere, reciprocal collaboration over strategic bargaining. We do not want money, systems, or rigid rules to prescribe a single path for everyone.

Two days later, let’s release sky lanterns together

Both experience options include admission to this sky lantern festival.

Two days after our Loy Krathong celebration, we will gather at CMI Studio for the sky lantern festival. We have our own little space where we can release sky lanterns. There is no crowd to squeeze through, just this group of us watching the lanterns rise slowly into the sky.

05 A final word

For last year’s Loy Krathong, more than ten of us travelled to the ancient city of Sukhothai in two pickup trucks. Sukhothai is best known for its sky lantern releases.

That night, lanterns rose from among the ancient ruins and filled the night sky. We stood beneath them, overwhelmed by the sight. In that moment, with palms together and a wish in mind, I felt wonderfully calm.

Amazingly, many of the wishes we made that year really did come true!

This year, we have our own lawn and pond, and another rare chance to meet. Since we have this celebration, why don’t we give it a try together? There is little time left to prepare, but I believe that meeting and working together will give each of us something new. I believe in the kindness each of us brings, and in the trust we share.

Want to hear the stories behind those wishes? Come and join us.
